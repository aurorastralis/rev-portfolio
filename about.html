About Me

I’m a Toronto-based copywriter, and I was named as Creative Face to Watch in 2023 by Campaign Middle East for my copy over the years.

I’ve also worked as a contributing editor for View the VIBE, where I’ve written about anything and everything to do with food, events, and lifestyle.

In fact, my obsession with the city’s F&B scene is what got me started into making content on social media. Especially for coffee shops and cafes. And I probably side-quested too hard, seeing as I’m in the top 5% of coffee and tea rankers in Toronto on the Beli app.

That’s all, folks!
